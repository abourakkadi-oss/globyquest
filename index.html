Globy Quest

Privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

Globy Quest is a geography game for children aged 7 to 10. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send any information over the internet. Everything a child does in the game stays on the device.

What the app keeps on the device

To show progress, the app saves the following on the phone or tablet only:

  • the player names typed in when a player is created (a nickname is fine);
  • scores, game history, badges, ranks and finished lessons for each player;
  • the answers to "Did you enjoy it?" after a game (a smiley rating).

This information never leaves the device, and we cannot see it.

What the app does not collect

  • No email addresses, phone numbers, photos, contacts or location.
  • No advertising identifiers, analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No third-party advertising or tracking code of any kind.
  • No in-app purchases and no links to social networks.

Feedback

The "Help improve" button is behind a grown-ups' check. If a parent chooses to send feedback, the app opens the device's own email app with a pre-written message. That message includes the app version, the device type and system version, and the smiley ratings. Nothing is sent unless the parent presses send in their email app. We use feedback emails only to improve the game and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Deleting information

A grown-up can delete a player in the app under "Grown-ups" on the "Who's playing?" screen, which removes that player's name, scores and badges. Deleting the app removes everything it saved.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information and sends nothing over the internet, it is designed to be used by children without a parent having to share any data with us.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. For any question about privacy, write to abourakkadi@gmail.com.
